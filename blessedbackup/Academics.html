Academic Programmes

Our curriculum blends strong academic subjects with practical learning to prepare students for A-Level success and a future career.

O-Level Programme

Our O-Level curriculum builds a strong foundation in core subjects while encouraging creativity, critical thinking and character development.

  • English Language & Literature
  • Mathematics
  • Science: Biology, Chemistry, Physics
  • Humanities: History & Geography
  • Creative Arts & ICT

A-Level Programme

At A-Level, students choose pathways that prepare them for university and careers with in-depth study and practical application.

  • Science combinations: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
  • Arts combinations: History, Economics, Literature
  • Business combinations: Accounting, Entrepreneurship, ICT
  • Research projects and guided study support

Science Department

Our science department offers well-equipped labs and hands-on experiments to foster scientific inquiry and confidence.

  • Biology with practical investigation
  • Chemistry lab work and safety training
  • Physics experiments and applied mathematics
  • Integrated STEM learning opportunities

ICT & Technology

We teach digital skills that students need for modern learning, future careers and responsible online citizenship.

  • Computer Studies and programming basics
  • Digital literacy and information systems
  • Multimedia, design and presentation tools
  • Project work using classroom technology

Sports & Co-curricular

Sport, clubs and co-curricular activities develop leadership, teamwork and healthy living beyond the classroom.

  • Football, athletics and netball
  • Debating, drama and music
  • Art, science club and nature studies
  • Community service and leadership programs

Lugazi Progressive Secondary School

Quality secondary education in Buikwe District since 1998. We nurture students with knowledge, discipline, leadership and service.
